MOONWELL: THE LOST LANTERN

A storm scattered the Lost Lantern's moonlight through the dream forest. Gather its firefly fragments and carry them home before dawn.

The Lost Lantern has gone quiet.

You are its young keeper, returning after the storm. The wandering fireflies are scattered moonlight—gather them, cross Moonroot Crossing, and reach Moonwell's heart to relight the lantern before dawn.

Moonflowers answer the moonlight; seek their cool glow. Arrow keys/W A S D walk. Press F to talk to Eir. In Whispering Hollow, press E to call an echo. Press P or Esc to pause; J rereads the memories you find.
