The Moonwell

A small well with a large memory. It wakes when its lantern is fed three lights, and it is particularly fond of patient walkers.

LOOK FOR · the quiet lantern
✦

Lantern motes

They drift where the trees leave room for moonlight. In the glade they will join your cloak when you get close. Three are enough to open the garden path.

GATHER · by walking near
✹

Gravity seed

Reusable, pink, and a little dramatic. In the Pocket Gravity Garden, place it once; use it again and each loose mote takes one square-step toward its pull.

USE · Space or the ✦ button
Keeper’s route
  1. Find the three fireflies in the glade.
  2. Walk to the lantern to enter the Pocket Gravity Garden.
  3. Place, then wake, the gravity seed until the motes reach you.
