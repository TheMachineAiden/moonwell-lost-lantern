The way home

Luna wants to get home after the storm. Her grandmother Eir is waiting with two travellers. Eir’s dropped lantern can help them find a safe path together.

Walk and look

Use the arrow keys or W A S D, or drag on the steering circle. Release or return to its centre to stop; tap a direction for a short step. Turn your phone sideways for a wider forest view. Near a mark, a person or a path opening, the action button tells you what you can do. Press F or tap that button.

Read each line at your own pace, then choose Continue or press Enter. Pause with P or Escape. You can restart from the pause menu.

One lantern, three sparks

Pick up Eir’s lantern in the north glade. Bring its three scattered sparks back into the glass. They are little pieces of light. You won’t need to collect more in the later areas.

Two ends, lit together

At the river, Eir holds a fixed light on the south bank. Luna lights the north mark with the lantern. When both ends are lit together, the old bridge holds again. Only Luna needs to cross it.

A repaired connection keeps its light. Going back, recalling an echo or picking up the lantern won’t undo a completed path.

Let your light wait

In the hollow, choose Shine here at the near stone. The lantern keeps the steps you actually walk. The route around the fallen trunk is already passable.

Create echo with E or the echo button. A light follows those recorded steps backwards and waits at the start, with no time limit. You can also create it while still standing at the start. There is no special place you must cast from.

Walk to the far stone with the real lantern and choose Shine here. Both ends must be lit at once. The new connection follows your walked route.

While an echo exists, its button says Recall echo. That action only recalls it. Press again to create another, or return to the start mark to begin a new recording. There is only one echo at a time.

At the Moonwell

Start a recording at the village mark, then walk to the well ring. Let an echo return to the village mark and put the real lantern in the well ring. The final chime answers only when both ends are lit together.

If you take the lantern too soon, the well ring goes dark. Put it back while the echo waits. If you recall the echo before the connection holds, take the lantern and try again from the village mark. Your bridge and hollow repairs stay safe.

Afterwards, take the lantern back to the child at the wet root. Shine on the next footing and wait. If you move away, the child waits too. Return when you are ready. Then walk home with Eir.

Last autumn

A folded leaf, a silver thread and an empty seed shell recall another walk with Grandma. They are optional. Open the notebook with J or choose Notebook from Pause to reread the memories you find.

There is no countdown. No one is left behind because you stopped to look.
